Appellation · Rhône Valley

Laudun

The 18th cru of the Rhône Valley, red and white since the 2024 vintage, in three communes of the Gard.

Grape varieties

Reds: Grenache and Syrah as the main grape varieties. Whites: Clairette and Grenache Blanc, with Roussanne or Viognier.

Terroir

Plateaux of limestone and safres, pebble terraces, in Laudun-l’Ardoise, Saint-Victor-la-Coste and Tresques.

Typical style

Fresh, fruity reds; the whites play an important part in the cru’s identity.

Aromas often mentioned: cherry, pepper, white flowers, pear.

Production rules

The cru covers only reds and whites: rosé is not part of it. Reds: Grenache and Syrah at least 75% of plantings (Grenache at least 50%, Syrah at least 25%). Whites: Grenache Blanc and Clairette at least 25% each, Viognier or Roussanne between 15 and 30%. Before becoming a cru, Laudun had been, since 1999, a complementary denomination of the Côtes du Rhône Villages AOC.

Ageing

Often 3 to 10 years.

Food pairing

  • Grilled fish (white)
  • lamb
  • goat’s cheese

Did you know?

The cru’s specification was approved on 21 November 2024; 2024 is the cru’s first vintage.

Communes of the area (3)
