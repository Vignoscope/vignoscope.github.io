Sub-region · Loire Valley

Auvergne, Forez et Roannais

The vineyards of the upper Loire and the Allier: Gamay and Chardonnay on volcanic and granite soils.

Open in the interactive atlas

Going up the Loire and the Allier towards the Massif Central, you find vineyards that are modest in size but very old. The Côte Roannaise and the Côtes du Forez grow Gamay on granite and volcanic soils.

The Côtes d’Auvergne, around Clermont-Ferrand, combine Gamay, Pinot Noir and Chardonnay on the volcanic hillsides of the Limagne; Saint-Pourçain, in the Allier, keeps a local white grape, Tressallier.

Appellations
