Grape varieties

Mourvèdre (at least 50% of the reds), Grenache, Cinsault; Clairette, Ugni Blanc, Bourboulenc for whites.

Terroir

Limestone, marl and sandstone on dry-stone terraces (“restanques”) forming an amphitheatre.

Very sunny, moderated by the sea: Mourvèdre, a late-ripening grape, finds the heat it needs here.

Typical style

Powerful, tannic reds with notes of leather, garrigue and black fruit; structured rosés that can age.

Aromas often mentioned: blackberry, liquorice, leather, garrigue.

Production rules

The reds must be aged for at least eighteen months in cask.

Ageing

Reds: 8 to 25 years; rosés: 2 to 5 years.

Food pairing

  • Provençal daube
  • game
  • rabbit with olives

Did you know?

Bandol has been an AOC since 1941.
