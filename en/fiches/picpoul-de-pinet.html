Grape varieties

Piquepoul Blanc.

Terroir

Limestone, clay and sand around the Thau lagoon.

Typical style

A lively, saline dry white, with notes of citrus and flowers.

Aromas often mentioned: lemon, grapefruit, iodine, flowers.

Ageing

1 to 3 years.

Food pairing

  • Bouzigues oysters
  • tielle sétoise (octopus pie)
  • mussels

Did you know?

The grape’s name, “lip-stinger”, is said to allude to its liveliness; Picpoul de Pinet has been an AOC since 2013.
