Grape varieties

Chenin (with a little Orbois tolerated).

Terroir

Flinty clay over tuffeau chalk, riddled with cellars where the wines age.

Typical style

From dry to sweet depending on the year, and sparkling wines (mousseux and pétillant): tension, quince, honey and great ageing potential.

Aromas often mentioned: quince, apple, honey, linden.

Ageing

Dry: 5 to 15 years; sweet: 20 years and more.
