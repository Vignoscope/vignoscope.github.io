Carignan, Grenache, Syrah, Mourvèdre.

Terroir

Schist, granite, gneiss and limestone of the Agly valley and the Aspres.

Typical style

Dense, spicy, warm reds.

Aromas often mentioned: blackberry, liquorice, garrigue, cocoa.

Production rules

Denominations: Caramany, Latour-de-France, Lesquerde, Tautavel and Les Aspres.

Ageing

4 to 12 years.
