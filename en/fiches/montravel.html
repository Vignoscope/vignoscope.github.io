Grape varieties

Sauvignon, Sémillon, Muscadelle for whites; mostly Merlot for reds.

Terroir

Clay-limestone hillsides and Dordogne terraces.

Typical style

Fresh, aromatic dry whites; fleshy reds.

Aromas often mentioned: citrus, flowers, plum.

Ageing

2 to 8 years.

Food pairing

  • Fish
  • poultry
  • duck breast

Did you know?

Michel de Montaigne was born at the château de Montaigne, in the Montravel.
