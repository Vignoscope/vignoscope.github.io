Grape varieties

Syrah, Grenache and Mourvèdre (at least 90% between them).

Terroir

Limestone and marl between two spectacular landforms, the Pic Saint-Loup and the Hortus cliff.

One of the coolest and wettest sectors of the Languedoc, with wide differences between day and night.

Typical style

Fresh, spicy, elegant reds with fine tannins.

Aromas often mentioned: blackberry, pepper, garrigue, violet.

Production rules

Reds and rosés; the area’s whites are released as AOC Languedoc.

Ageing

4 to 12 years.

Food pairing

  • Lamb
  • game birds
  • pélardon (goat’s cheese)

Did you know?

Pic Saint-Loup became an AOC in its own right in 2017.
