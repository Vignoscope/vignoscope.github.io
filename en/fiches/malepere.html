Grape varieties

Mostly Merlot and Cabernet Franc, with Côt, Cabernet Sauvignon, Grenache and Syrah.

Terroir

Sandstone and clay hills of the Malepère massif.

Typical style

Supple, fruity reds, close to Bordeaux.

Aromas often mentioned: blackcurrant, cherry, green pepper.

Ageing

3 to 8 years.
