Grape varieties

Riesling, Gewurztraminer, Pinot Gris and Muscat; a few Grands Crus allow other grape varieties or blends.

Terroir

Volcano-sedimentary rocks on dizzying slopes, on the banks of the Thur.

Typical style

Smoky, powerful Riesling, Pinot Gris and Gewurztraminer with very long ageing potential.

Ageing

8 to 25 years, longer for vendanges tardives.

Food pairing

  • Choucroute or fish cooked in Riesling
  • Munster cheese with Gewurztraminer
  • poultry in cream sauce

Did you know?

The Rangen is the southernmost Alsace Grand Cru; the Clos Saint-Urbain, owned by Domaine Zind-Humbrecht, occupies its heart.

Communes of the area (2)

Thann, Vieux-Thann.
