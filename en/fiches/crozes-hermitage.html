Practise: 6 questions drawn from the checked facts

Terroir

In the north, granite hillsides close to Hermitage (Gervans, Erôme); in the south, the Chassis plain, terraces of glacial alluvium covered with rolled pebbles.

The flatter, warmer southern sector gives fruity, early-drinking wines; the northern hillsides, tauter wines for ageing.

Typical style

A moreish, fresh Syrah: blackcurrant, blackberry, violet, pepper, smoky notes; supple tannins, to drink young or after a few years.

Aromas often mentioned: blackcurrant, blackberry, violet, pepper, smoke.

Production rules

Reds: Syrah, with up to 15% Marsanne and Roussanne. Whites: Marsanne and Roussanne.

Ageing

3 to 10 years; up to 15 years for the best cuvées.

Food pairing

  • Wood-fired grilled meats
  • Ardèche caillette
  • Picodon (Drôme goat’s cheese)

Did you know?

The name comes from the village of Crozes-Hermitage, north of Tain; the vineyard spreads over eleven communes around the famous hill.

Communes of the area (11)
