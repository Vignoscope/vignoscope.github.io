Grape varieties

Gamay Noir à Jus Blanc.

Terroir

Diorite, known as “pierre bleue” (blue stone), on the flanks of an ancient volcano.

Typical style

Reds more concentrated and more mineral than Brouilly.

Aromas often mentioned: cherry, iris, pepper, stone.

Ageing

3 to 8 years.

Food pairing

  • Lyon charcuterie
  • coq au vin
  • Bresse chicken
  • goat’s cheeses

Did you know?

At the top of Mont Brouilly, the chapel of Notre-Dame-du-Raisin, built in the 19th century, hosts a winegrowers’ pilgrimage.

Communes of the area (4)

Cercié, Odenas, Quincié-en-Beaujolais, Saint-Lager.
