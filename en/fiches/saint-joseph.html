Appellation · Rhône Valley

Saint-Joseph

The longest cru of the northern Rhône: some fifty kilometres of hillsides on the right bank, from Chavanay to Guilherand-Granges.

Grape varieties

Syrah for reds (up to 10% Marsanne and Roussanne); Marsanne and Roussanne for whites.

Terroir

Granite and gneiss hillsides over some fifty kilometres, often terraced.

Typical style

Fruity, peppery reds, suppler than Hermitage or Cornas; rounded, floral whites.

Aromas often mentioned: blackcurrant, raspberry, pepper, violet.

Ageing

4 to 12 years.

Food pairing

  • Caillette
  • leg of lamb
  • picodon (goat’s cheese)

Did you know?

The name is said to come from a hillside in Tournon that once belonged to the Jesuits.

Communes of the area (26)
