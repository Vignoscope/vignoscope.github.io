Grape varieties

Petit Manseng and Gros Manseng, with Courbu, Camaralet and Lauzet.

Terroir

Hillsides of puddingstone, clay and limestone south of Pau, often terraced.

The foehn, a warm, dry wind from Spain, encourages the grapes to dry on the vine in autumn.

Typical style

Vibrant sweet wines in which sugar is balanced by lively acidity; a taut, exotic Jurançon sec.

Aromas often mentioned: pineapple, mango, candied citrus, honey.

Ageing

Sweet: 5 to 30 years; dry: 2 to 8 years.

Food pairing

  • Foie gras
  • sheep’s cheeses
  • exotic-fruit desserts

Did you know?

According to tradition, the lips of the future Henri IV were rubbed with garlic and Jurançon wine at his birth in 1553.
