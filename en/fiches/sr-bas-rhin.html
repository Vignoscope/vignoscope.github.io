Sub-region · Alsace

Bas-Rhin

The north of the Alsace vineyard, from Marlenheim to Saint-Hippolyte: varied terroirs and 14 Grands Crus.

Open in the interactive atlas

The Bas-Rhin accounts for about a third of the Alsace vineyard. The sub-Vosges hills are less sheltered here than in the south, which often gives livelier wines.

The soils are very diverse: sandstone, granite, schist and limestone. The département’s 14 Grands Crus run from the Steinklotz of Marlenheim to the Frankstein of Dambach-la-Ville; the Zotzenberg of Mittelbergheim is the only Grand Cru where Sylvaner is allowed.
