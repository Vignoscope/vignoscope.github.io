Sub-region · Loire Valley

Centre-Loire

In the centre of France: the Sauvignon of Sancerre and Pouilly-Fumé, on flint, marl and limestone.

Open in the interactive atlas

The Centre-Loire brings together vineyards scattered around the Loire and its tributaries. Sancerre and Pouilly-Fumé, on either side of the river, made Sauvignon Blanc famous worldwide; Menetou-Salon, Quincy, Reuilly and the Coteaux du Giennois keep them company.

Pinot Noir produces light reds and rosés here. Châteaumeillant, further south, is devoted to Gamay; Orléans and Orléans-Cléry carry on a vineyard that once spread widely around the city.
