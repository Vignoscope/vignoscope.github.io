Grape varieties

Chardonnay.

Terroir

White marl and limestone at the top of the hill of Corton.

Typical style

Tension, minerality and power; the best need ten years to open out.

Aromas often mentioned: lemon, hazelnut, gunflint, honey (with age).

Ageing

12 to 30 years.

Food pairing

  • Lobster or crayfish
  • Bresse chicken in cream sauce
  • turbot with beurre blanc

Did you know?

According to tradition, the Emperor Charlemagne had white vines planted on the hill so as not to stain his beard with red wine.
