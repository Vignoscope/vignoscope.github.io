Quarts de Chaume

Grape varieties

Chenin.

Terroir

Schist and sandstone on a small plateau sloping towards the Layon, very well exposed to the south.

Typical style

Sweet wines of great concentration, fresh and with very long ageing potential.

Aromas often mentioned: candied quince, apricot, honey, saffron.

Production rules

The term Grand Cru since 2011; very low yields and grapes picked in successive passes.

Ageing

15 to 50 years.

Food pairing

  • Foie gras
  • blue cheeses
  • yellow-fruit desserts

Did you know?

The name is said to recall that in the Middle Ages the local lord kept a quarter (quart) of the harvest, the best part, for himself.
