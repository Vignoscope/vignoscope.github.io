Grape varieties

Gamay and Pinot Noir for reds and rosés; Chardonnay for whites.

Terroir

Basalt flows, peperites and limestone on the buttes of the Limagne.

Typical style

Fruity, peppery reds, fresh rosés.

Aromas often mentioned: cherry, raspberry, pepper.

Production rules

Five denominations: Boudes, Chanturgue, Chateaugay, Corent and Madargues.

Ageing

2 to 6 years.

Food pairing

  • Truffade
  • Auvergne potée
  • Saint-Nectaire

Did you know?

The Auvergne vineyard covered several tens of thousands of hectares before phylloxera; the Côtes d’Auvergne have been an AOC since 2011.

Recognised denominations

Côtes d'Auvergne Boudes ; Côtes d'Auvergne Chanturgue ; Côtes d'Auvergne Chateaugay ; Côtes d'Auvergne Corent ; Côtes d'Auvergne Madargues.
