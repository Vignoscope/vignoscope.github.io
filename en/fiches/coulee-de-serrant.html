Grape varieties

Chenin.

Terroir

Schist and rhyolite on a steep slope running down to the river.

Typical style

A dry Chenin of great intensity, with notes of quince, honey and dried fruit.

Aromas often mentioned: quince, honey, dried fruit and nuts, spices.

Ageing

10 to 30 years.

Food pairing

  • Loué poultry
  • fine fish
  • matured cheeses

Did you know?

The Coulée de Serrant, planted by the Cistercians in the 12th century, belongs to the Joly family; Nicolas Joly has been a pioneer of biodynamics there since the 1980s.
