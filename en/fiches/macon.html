Grape varieties

Chardonnay for whites; Gamay and Pinot Noir for reds and rosés.

Terroir

Limestone and marl of the Mâconnais hills, clay and sand of the plain.

Typical style

Rounded, fruity whites; supple, light Gamay reds.

Aromas often mentioned: white peach, flowers, cherry (reds).

Production rules

The name of a village may follow that of Mâcon (Mâcon-Lugny, Mâcon-Pierreclos, Mâcon-Verzé…); Mâcon-Villages is reserved for whites.

Ageing

1 to 5 years.

Food pairing

  • Quenelles
  • poultry
  • Mâconnais goat’s cheeses
