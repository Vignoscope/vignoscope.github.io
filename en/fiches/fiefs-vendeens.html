Grape varieties

Pinot Noir, Gamay, Cabernet Franc, Négrette for reds; Chenin, Chardonnay, Sauvignon for whites.

Terroir

Schist, granite and limestone depending on the sector: Brem, Chantonnay, Mareuil, Pissotte and Vix.

Typical style

Fresh, light wines, fruity rosés and reds, lively whites.

Aromas often mentioned: strawberry, cherry, citrus.

Ageing

1 to 4 years.

Food pairing

  • Vendée ham
  • seafood
  • mogettes (white beans)

Communes of the area (18)

Auchay-sur-Vendée, Brem-sur-Mer, Bretignolles-sur-Mer, Le Champ-Saint-Père, Chantonnay, Château-Guibert, La Couture, Longèves, Mareuil-sur-Lay-Dissais, Pissotte, Le Poiré-sur-Velluire, Rives de l'Yon, Rosnay, Les Sables-d'Olonne, Le Tablier, Vairé, Vix, L'Île-d'Olonne.
