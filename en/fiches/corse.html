Regions

Wine region

Corsica

A mountain in the sea: wines of sun and maquis from island grape varieties.

Open in the interactive atlas

Leading grape varieties
Nielluccio, Sciaccarellu, Vermentinu

Mediterranean, very sunny, tempered by the sea and by altitude; frequent winds.

Geology and soils

Granite in the west and south (Ajaccio, Sartène, Figari), schist in the north-east (Cap Corse), a limestone island at Patrimonio.

Taste profile

Spicy, fragrant reds (maquis, pepper, red fruit), fresh rosés, Vermentinu whites, Muscat du Cap Corse.

Appellations

  • Vin de Corse
  • Ajaccio
  • Patrimonio
  • Muscat du Cap Corse
