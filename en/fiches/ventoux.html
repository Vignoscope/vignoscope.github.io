Grape varieties

Grenache, Syrah, Cinsault, Mourvèdre, Carignan; Clairette, Bourboulenc, Grenache Blanc, Roussanne for whites.

Terroir

Limestone, sand and clay hillsides at the foot of the “Giant of Provence”.

Typical style

Fruity, fresh reds and rosés, to drink young.

Aromas often mentioned: strawberry, cherry, pepper, herbs.

Ageing

2 to 5 years.

Food pairing

  • Pizza
  • grilled meats
  • petits farcis (stuffed vegetables)

Did you know?

The appellation, created in 1973 as Côtes du Ventoux, has been called Ventoux since 2009.
