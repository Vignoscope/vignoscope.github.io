Grape varieties

Mainly Sauvignon Blanc; the Cabernets, Gamay and Pinot Noir for reds.

Terroir

Limestone and clay plateau around Neuville-de-Poitou.

Typical style

Lively, aromatic dry whites; light reds.

Aromas often mentioned: grapefruit, boxwood, red fruit.

Ageing

1 to 4 years.

Food pairing

  • Chabichou cheese
  • fish
  • grilled meats

Did you know?

Haut-Poitou has been an AOC since 2011.

Communes of the area (23)
