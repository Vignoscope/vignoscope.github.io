Terroir

A granite amphitheatre on the right bank, facing Valence, with steep slopes held up by low walls. Poor, warm soils of granite sand.

The natural bowl shelters the vines from the mistral: it is one of the warmest sectors of the northern Rhône, hence the ripeness and power of the wines.

Typical style

Inky colour; blackberry, blackcurrant, violet, pepper, liquorice, wild and smoky notes. A dense palate, firm, rustic tannins in youth that mellow after about ten years.

Aromas often mentioned: blackberry, blackcurrant, black pepper, liquorice, smoke.

Production rules

100% Syrah, with no white grape at all, unlike Côte-Rôtie or Hermitage.

Ageing

10 to 30 years.

Food pairing

  • Wild boar stew
  • Provençal daube
  • Côte de bœuf grilled over vine cuttings

Did you know?

The name Cornas is said to come from a Celtic word meaning “burnt earth”.
