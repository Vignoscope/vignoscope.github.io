Grape varieties

Gamay Noir à Jus Blanc.

Terroir

Manganese-rich pink granite, around an old windmill.

Typical style

Deep, structured reds that take on notes of undergrowth with age and sometimes recall Burgundy.

Aromas often mentioned: black cherry, iris, spices, undergrowth.

Ageing

5 to 15 years.

Food pairing

  • Lyon charcuterie
  • coq au vin
  • Bresse chicken
  • goat’s cheeses

Did you know?

The cru owes its name to the old windmill that overlooks the vines of Romanèche-Thorins.
