Grape varieties

Grenache, Syrah, Mourvèdre, Carignan, Cinsault; Grenache Blanc, Roussanne, Marsanne, Clairette, Vermentino for whites.

Terroir

Terraces of rolled pebbles deposited by the ancient Rhône and the Durance.

Mediterranean, tempered by sea breezes from the Camargue.

Typical style

Fruity, supple reds, fresh rosés and whites.

Aromas often mentioned: red fruit, pepper, garrigue.

Ageing

2 to 8 years.

Food pairing

  • Camargue bull
  • brandade
  • grilled meats
