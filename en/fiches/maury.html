Grape varieties

Mainly Grenache Noir.

Terroir

Black schist in the heart of the Agly valley.

Typical style

Deep sweet wines with notes of black fruit, cocoa and rancio; dry Maury is a powerful, mineral red.

Aromas often mentioned: blackberry, cocoa, coffee, fig.

Production rules

Vin doux naturel: fermentation is stopped by adding neutral alcohol (mutage), which keeps part of the grape sugar.

Ageing

10 to 40 years.

Food pairing

  • Chocolate desserts
  • blue cheeses
  • game (dry)

Did you know?

Since 2011, the Maury appellation has also produced dry reds.

Communes of the area (4)

Maury, Rasiguères, Saint-Paul-de-Fenouillet, Tautavel.
