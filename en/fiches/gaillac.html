Gaillac

One of the oldest vineyards in France, rich in native grape varieties and varied styles.

Grape varieties

Duras, Braucol (Fer Servadou), Prunelart, Syrah for reds; Mauzac, Len de l’El (Loin de l’œil), Ondenc, Sauvignon for whites.

Terroir

Left bank of the Tarn: gravel and terraces; right bank: limestone hillsides and the Cordes plateau.

Typical style

Peppery reds, dry or sweet whites, sparkling wines made by the ancestral method known as “gaillacoise”, and wines aged under a veil of yeast.

Aromas often mentioned: apple, pear, pepper, red fruit.

Ageing

1 to 10 years depending on the style.

Food pairing

  • Cassoulet
  • Toulouse sausage
  • apple tart (sweet)

Did you know?

Excavations have shown that vines were grown and wine produced in Gaillac as early as Roman times.
