Grape varieties

Chardonnay.

Terroir

Limestone and marl on south-facing hills, east of the Cure valley.

Typical style

A fine, taut dry white, with notes of citrus and flowers.

Aromas often mentioned: lemon, white flowers, fresh almond.

Ageing

2 to 6 years.

Food pairing

  • Trout
  • chicken with morels
  • goat’s cheese

Did you know?

Vézelay became a village AOC in 2017; the hill and its basilica are on the UNESCO World Heritage list.
