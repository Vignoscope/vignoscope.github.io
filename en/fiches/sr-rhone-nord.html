Sub-region · Rhône Valley

Rhône septentrional

From Vienne to Valence: steep granite slopes, Syrah for reds, Viognier, Marsanne and Roussanne for whites.

Open in the interactive atlas

Over about 70 km, the northern Rhône lines up eight crus on terraced slopes: Côte-Rôtie, Condrieu, Château-Grillet, Saint-Joseph, Hermitage, Crozes-Hermitage, Cornas and Saint-Péray.

The climate is temperate continental, and the vines seek south or south-east exposures. Syrah is the only red grape; the whites are based on Viognier (Condrieu, Château-Grillet) or on Marsanne and Roussanne.
