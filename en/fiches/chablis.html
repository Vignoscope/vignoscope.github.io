Appellation · Burgundy

Chablis

The mineral Chardonnay of the Yonne, on marl with fossil oysters; the AOC includes the Premiers Crus.

Open in the interactive atlas

In brief

The Chablis AOC covers only still white wines, made exclusively from Chardonnay. The name may be followed by “premier cru” and by the name of a climat.

  • About forty premier cru climats, grouped under 17 names.
  • Minimum natural alcohol: 10% (10.5% for the premiers crus).

Practise: 5 questions drawn from the checked facts

Terroir

Kimmeridgian: grey marl and limestone full of tiny fossil oysters. The seven Grands Crus (Blanchot, Bougros, Les Clos, Grenouilles, Preuses, Valmur, Vaudésir) form a single south-west-facing slope, facing the town, on the right bank of the Serein. Petit Chablis grows on the younger Portlandian.

A cool semi-continental climate, at the northern limit of Chardonnay. Spring frost is the growers’ nightmare: candles, water sprinkling and anti-frost towers protect the buds.

Typical style

Tension and purity: lemon, green apple, white flowers, oyster shell, gunflint. The Grands Crus add breadth, then honey and hazelnut with age, without losing their precision.

Aromas often mentioned: lemon, green apple, oyster shell, flint, white flowers.

Production rules

100% Chardonnay. Often vinified in stainless steel to preserve freshness; the Premiers and Grands Crus often go into used oak casks, sometimes feuillettes.

Ageing

Chablis: 3 to 8 years; Premiers Crus: 5 to 15 years; Grands Crus: 10 to 25 years.

Food pairing

  • Oysters and seafood
  • Burgundy snails
  • Sole meunière
  • Chaource

Did you know?

The Cistercian monks of the nearby abbey of Pontigny contributed greatly to the development of the Chablis vineyard from the 12th century.
