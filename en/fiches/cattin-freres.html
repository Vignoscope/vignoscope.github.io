Estate · Alsace grand cru Hatschbourg

Cattin Frères (Maison Cattin)

A family in Vœgtlinshoffen since 1660, now one of the great houses of southern Alsace, at the foot of the Hatschbourg grand cru.

Open in the interactive atlas

Appellations: Alsace grand cru Hatschbourg, Crémant d'Alsace, Alsace

History

Originally from the Swiss Jura, the family settled in Vœgtlinshoffen in 1660, planted its first vines in 1720 and devoted itself to winegrowing from 1850. The brothers Jacques and Jean-Marie took over the estate in 1978 and developed a merchant business; the next generation arrived in 2007.

Vineyard

Nearly 90 ha, including plots in the Hatschbourg grand cru; part of the vineyard is converting to organic farming.

Winemaking

Crémants aged in particular for 18 months sur lattes; vendanges tardives and sélections de grains nobles in some years.
