Sub-region · Rhône Valley

Diois

In the Drôme valley, at the foot of the Vercors: the sparkling wines of Die and mountain wines.

Open in the interactive atlas

The Diois is a high-altitude vineyard, roughly between 200 and 700 m, isolated in the Drôme valley. It is best known for Clairette de Die, a Muscat-based sparkling wine made by the méthode dioise ancestrale.

Crémant de Die, Coteaux de Die (still Clairette) and Châtillon-en-Diois (Gamay, Pinot Noir, Aligoté, Chardonnay) complete the range.

Appellations

  • Clairette de Die
  • Crémant de Die
  • Coteaux de Die
  • Châtillon-en-Diois
