Grape varieties

Gamay for reds and rosés; Chardonnay and Aligoté for whites.

Terroir

Granite, schist and alluvium of the Monts du Lyonnais.

Typical style

Light, fruity wines to drink young, the house wine of Lyon’s bouchons.

Aromas often mentioned: strawberry, cherry, apple.

Ageing

1 to 3 years.

Food pairing

  • Sausage in brioche
  • tablier de sapeur (breaded tripe)
  • cervelle de canut (herbed cheese spread)

Did you know?

The Coteaux du Lyonnais have been an AOC since 1984.
