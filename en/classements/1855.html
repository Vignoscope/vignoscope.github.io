Classification · Médoc, Graves and Sauternes-Barsac

The 1855 classification

Drawn up by Bordeaux brokers for the Paris Universal Exhibition, based on the prices of the wines. Apart from the addition of Cantemerle in 1856, it has been changed only once, in 1973, when Mouton Rothschild was promoted to Premier Cru (First Growth).

Good to know

Château Haut-Brion, in the Graves, is the only château outside the Médoc classified among the reds.

Names may have changed since 1855; Château Dubignon, a third growth, was absorbed by a neighbour.
