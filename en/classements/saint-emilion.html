The Saint-Émilion classification (2022)

The only Bordeaux classification revised regularly, roughly every ten years. The 2022 edition has 85 Grands Crus Classés, 14 of them Premiers Grands Crus Classés.

Good to know

The 71 other châteaux are Grands Crus Classés.

The classification applies to the Saint-Émilion Grand Cru appellation, which is distinct from the Saint-Émilion appellation.
